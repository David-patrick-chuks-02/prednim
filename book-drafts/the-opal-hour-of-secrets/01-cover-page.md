**Where secrets hide in plain sight, and truth demands a terrible price.**

In the shadows of a bustling city, a rare opal holds the power to transform destinies. When Marcus Hartwell's carefully constructed world begins to crumble, he discovers that some secrets—no matter how well buried—inevitably emerge. And when they do, everyone it touches must choose: defend the lie, or face the truth.

A gripping tale of love, obsession, murder, and the possibility of redemption spanning over a century.
