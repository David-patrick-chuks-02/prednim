## Reading Guide

This novel spans over a century and weaves together multiple narratives and perspectives. Readers are encouraged to pay attention to the recurring themes of consciousness, redemption, love, and the price of truth as they move through the story.

The opal itself serves as both a literal object and a metaphor for humanity's eternal search for meaning and transcendence.
