## About This Book

In a world where secrets are currency and truth is dangerous, one artifact holds the power to change everything.

The Opal Hour of Secrets is a mystery thriller spanning over a century, exploring themes of consciousness, redemption, love, and the cost of truth. When a rare opal surfaces in the possession of a wealthy businessman, it triggers a cascade of revelations that will ultimately expose a hidden organization and transform the lives of everyone who touches it.

Perfect for readers who enjoy:
- Complex character studies
- Psychological mysteries
- Themes of redemption and forgiveness
- Multi-generational narratives
- Questions about consciousness and identity
